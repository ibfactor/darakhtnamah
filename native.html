Native

Native plants are getting exceedingly difficult to find as people are moving towards imported ones.

Traditional drought-resistant plants, such as Jand (Prosopis cineraria), or Keekar (Acacia nilotica) have been replaced by conocarpus and eucalyptus, which are detrimental to both water table level and to the environment.

This website includes many native plant species suited to our habitat and environment, with the added benefit of helping the native fauna.
